# The Individual – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: The Individual**

- Description of external appearance
- Description of character and personality
- Fashion and trends
- Daily routine

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### External appearance

- How would you describe your own appearance?
- What do you notice first when you meet a new person?
- Do you think it is right to judge people by their appearance? Why / why not?
- Has your appearance changed a lot over the years?

### Character and personality

- Which three adjectives describe your personality best?
- Are you more like your mother or your father in character?
- What qualities do you appreciate most in your friends?
- Can people change their personality, or is it fixed?

### Fashion and trends

- Do you follow fashion trends? Why / why not?
- Is it important to wear branded clothes?
- How important is the way you dress at school or at work?
- Do you prefer shopping in stores or online? Why?

### Daily routine

- Describe your typical weekday from morning to evening.
- What is your favourite part of the day, and why?
- What would you like to change about your daily routine?
- How do you spend your weekends differently from weekdays?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

- **First of all, … / To begin with, …** – Mindenekelőtt…
- **Another thing is that …** – Egy másik dolog az, hogy…
- **On the one hand … on the other hand …** – Egyrészt… másrészt…
- **What is more, … / Besides, …** – Ráadásul…
- **All things considered, …** – Mindent egybevetve…
- **In my opinion / Personally, I think …** – Véleményem szerint…
- **It depends on …** – Attól függ, hogy…

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about the individual: appearance, personality, fashion and daily routines.
>
> First of all, let me describe my appearance. I am of medium height and I have shoulder-length, curly brown hair. People say I take after my father — we have the same brown eyes and a similar smile. Personally, I do not judge people by their appearance, because clothes and looks can be misleading.
>
> As for my personality, I think I am an easy-going and reliable person. My friends say I have a good sense of humour, but sometimes I can be a bit stubborn, too. In a friend I value honesty and generosity the most.
>
> Speaking about fashion, I prefer casual, comfortable clothes rather than expensive brand names. I believe it is much more important that clothes fit well and suit your personality than following every new trend. However, I sometimes check what is in fashion online.
>
> Finally, my daily routine. On weekdays I get up at six-thirty, because I am an early bird. After school I do my homework, help my parents with the chores, and in the evening I unwind by listening to music or going for a walk. At weekends my schedule is more relaxed — I usually meet my friends.
>
> All things considered, I think a person is much more than their appearance: character, style and habits together make us who we are. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz (Example answers about someone else)

A vizsgán gyakran nem magadról, hanem egy családtagról, barátról vagy ismerősről kell beszélned. Íme példaválaszok mind a négy alpontra.

### a) Külső megjelenés – a legjobb barátnőd

> Let me describe my best friend, Anna. She is twenty years old and quite tall — she is about one hundred and seventy-five centimetres. She is slim and well-built because she does a lot of sport. She has got long, straight, dark brown hair, which she usually wears in a ponytail, and her eyes are green. She also has a small scar above her left eyebrow from a childhood accident. People say she takes after her mother. She usually dresses casually — jeans, trainers and a hoodie — but she always looks tidy.

### b) Jellelem – a nagypapád

> My grandfather is one of the most easy-going people I know. Although he is in his seventies, he still has a great sense of humour, and he always makes everyone laugh at family dinners. He is extremely generous: he is always ready to help the neighbours, and he never expects anything in return. He is also very reliable — if he promises something, he keeps his word. His only weak point is that he can be a bit stubborn: once he has made up his mind, nobody can change it.

### c) Divat – az osztálytársad és a nővéred (ellenpéldával)

> My classmate Bendegúz is really into fashion. He always wears the latest brands — expensive trainers, hoodies and designer T-shirts — and he checks online every day what is in fashion. My sister, on the other hand, doesn't care about trends at all. She prefers second-hand clothes, and she says fast fashion is bad for the environment. I am somewhere in between: I like nice clothes, but I wouldn't pay a fortune for a brand name.

### d) Napirend – az édesanyád

> Let me tell you about my mother's daily routine. She gets up at half past five — she is a real early bird. First she makes breakfast for the family, then she drives to work and she is at the office from eight to four. After work she runs errands, cooks dinner and helps me with my homework. In the evening she unwinds by reading or watching a series. At weekends her routine is much more relaxed: she sleeps in, and we often go shopping together.

### e) Egy teljes tétel egyetlen személyről – a nővéred

> Today I would like to talk about my older sister, Eszter, who is probably the most interesting person in my family.
>
> First, her appearance. She is in her mid-twenties, of medium height, with short curly hair and round glasses. She is slim, and she takes after our father — they have the same smile. She usually wears simple, comfortable clothes, mostly in dark colours.
>
> As for her character, she is very hard-working and self-confident. She studied at university while working at the same time, which I really admire. She is easy-going and generous, but she can be a little impatient when she is tired.
>
> When it comes to fashion, she used to follow every trend as a teenager, but now she says comfort is more important than brands. She mostly shops second-hand, because she thinks fast fashion harms the planet.
>
> Finally, her daily routine. She works as a nurse, so she works in shifts and no two days are the same. On a morning shift she gets up at five, and she is often home late in the evening. On her days off, she sleeps in, goes running and meets her friends.
>
> All things considered, my sister has taught me that a person is much more than their looks: her kindness and determination make her special. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- A mondatfelsorolás helyett használj összekötő kifejezéseket – a B szinten ez ad szerkezeti pontot.
- Mind a négy alpontról beszélj nagyjából azonos hosszan (kb. 1–1,5 perc részenként).
- A „Miért?" kérdésekre adj indoklást, ne csak tényeket sorolj.
- Ha nem ismersz egy szót, kerülő megfogalmazással mondd el (pl. *„a person who always arrives on time"* = punctual).
