# Human Relations – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Human relations**

- friendship
- school and work connections
- partnerships

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Friendship

- What makes a good friend?
- How did you meet your best friend?
- What do you like doing together?
- Is it easier to make friends online or in person? Why?

### School and work connections

- How do you get on with your classmates and teachers?
- Why is teamwork important at school or at work?
- Have you ever worked with someone you didn't like? What happened?
- What makes a good teacher–student relationship?

### Partnerships

- What do you think is the secret of a good relationship?
- What is your opinion about getting married young?
- Are long-distance relationships a good idea?
- How can people keep a relationship alive?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

- **First of all, … / To begin with, …** – Mindenekelőtt…
- **Another thing is that …** – Egy másik dolog az, hogy…
- **On the one hand … on the other hand …** – Egyrészt… másrészt…
- **What is more, … / Besides, …** – Ráadásul…
- **All things considered, …** – Mindent egybevetve…
- **In my opinion / Personally, I think …** – Véleményem szerint…
- **It depends on …** – Attól függ, hogy…

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about human relations: friendship, school and work connections, and partnerships.
>
> First of all, friendship. My best friend is Bendegúz. We met in the first year of primary school, when we sat next to each other, and we have kept in touch ever since. We have a lot in common: we both love football and the same music. In my opinion, a good friend is honest and reliable, someone you can rely on in difficult situations. Of course, we sometimes argue about silly things, but we always make up quickly.
>
> As for school connections, I get on well with my classmates, and our form teacher is strict but fair. I think group projects teach us teamwork, which will be important at work later. Last summer I had a student job in a shop, and my older colleagues always helped me when I got stuck, so I learned that good relationships make work easier.
>
> Speaking about partnerships, my parents have been married for twenty years, and I believe the secret of their relationship is that they trust each other and share the housework. My sister has been in a long-distance relationship for two years, because her boyfriend studies in another city, but it works: they video-chat every evening and meet at weekends.
>
> All things considered, I think every good relation — friendship, school or work — is based on honesty and trust. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Barátság – a legjobb barátod

> My best friend Bendegúz is the most reliable person I know. We met in primary school when we sat next to each other, and we hit it off immediately. He is easy-going and he has a great sense of humour, so he can always cheer me up. When I broke my leg last year, he visited me every week and brought me the homework — that is what I call a true friend. Of course, we sometimes fall out about football, but we always make up within a day.

### b) Iskolai és munkahelyi kapcsolatok – a nővéred

> My sister works as a nurse, and she always says that good colleagues are as important as good pay. In her hospital, teamwork is a matter of life and death: the nurses help each other during difficult shifts, and the doctors respect their opinion. Her colleagues often go out together after work, so the atmosphere is very friendly. Thanks to her, I have learned that when I choose a job, I should look at the people first, and only then at the salary.

### c) Párkapcsolat – a nagyszüleid

> My grandparents have been married for fifty years, and they still hold hands when they go for a walk. I once asked my grandmother about the secret of their marriage. She said they have never gone to sleep angry with each other, and they always talk about their problems calmly instead of shouting. My grandfather says patience and humour are the most important things in a relationship. I really admire them, and I hope I will have a marriage like theirs one day.

### d) Egy teljes tétel egyetlen személyről – a nővéred

> I would like to talk about my older sister Eszter, because human relations are very important in her life.
>
> First, friendship. Her best friend is Anna, whom she met at university. They live in different cities now, but they have kept in touch and meet every month.
>
> As for work connections, Eszter works as a nurse. She gets on well with her colleagues and her boss as well, and she says that at the hospital teamwork saves lives, so everybody has to rely on each other.
>
> Speaking about partnerships, she has been in a long-distance relationship for two years. Her boyfriend studies in Debrecen, so they video-chat every evening and travel to see each other at weekends.
>
> All things considered, my sister has taught me that if you are honest and reliable, you will never be alone. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- A „Why?" típusú kérdésekre mindig adj példát is – egy konkrét történet (pl. egy összeveszés és kibékülés) több pontot ér, mint a tulajdonságok felsorolása.
- Figyelj az ige + elöljáró szerkezetekre: *rely ON, get on WITH, fall out WITH, keep in touch WITH*.
- Használj jellemtulajdonságokat a szókincsből (honest, reliable, easy-going), de indokold is, miért fontosak neked.
- Keverheted az E/1-et és a másokról szóló részeket – a vizsgán pont az a természetes, ha a saját élményeidbe másokat is belesző.
