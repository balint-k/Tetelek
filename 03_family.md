# Family – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Family**

- family models (e.g. small and large families)
- family division of labour
- generations living together

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Family models

- How many people are there in your family?
- What are the advantages of growing up in a large family?
- What is it like to be an only child?
- Which family model is the most common in Hungary today?

### Family division of labour

- Who does what in your home?
- Should children help with the household chores? Why?
- Who is the breadwinner in your family?
- Do you think the division of housework is fair in most families?

### Generations living together

- Do your grandparents live near you or with you?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of three generations living together?
- How often do you meet your relatives?
- What can young people learn from the older generation?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

- **First of all, … / To begin with, …** – Mindenekelőtt…
- **Another thing is that …** – Egy másik dolog az, hogy…
- **On the one hand … on the other hand …** – Egyrészt… másrészt…
- **What is more, … / Besides, …** – Ráadásul…
- **All things considered, …** – Mindent egybevetve…
- **In my opinion / Personally, I think …** – Véleményem szerint…
- **It depends on …** – Attól függ, hogy…

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about families: family models, the division of labour at home, and generations living together.
>
> First of all, I live in a nuclear family: there are four of us — my parents, my younger sister and me. However, we have a huge extended family, with more than twenty cousins, so our Christmas parties are always loud and fun. In my opinion, children in large families are never bored and learn to share, while only children get more attention from their parents — but sometimes they feel lonely.
>
> As for the division of labour, in our home everybody does something. My mother cooks and does the washing-up, my father does the shopping and fixes things, I take out the rubbish and hoover my room, and my sister feeds the cat. I believe children should help at home, because it teaches them responsibility — and we get some pocket money for it, too.
>
> Finally, generations. My grandmother has lived with us since my grandfather passed away. It has many advantages: she cooks delicious food, helps with the chores and tells wonderful old stories. On the other hand, we have less privacy, and she sometimes still treats me like a small child.
>
> All things considered, I think a family is not about its size, but about how much its members love and help each other. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Családmodellek – a nagybátyád nagy családja

> My uncle's family is the complete opposite of ours: they have five children, so their house is never quiet. The older kids look after the younger ones and help in the kitchen, and the twins even share a room. The children don't get pocket money for chores — they help because it is natural in a big family. When I visit them, I am always amazed how well they share everything, from the bathroom in the morning to the last slice of pizza.

### b) Munkamegosztás – a barátod családjában

> In my friend Bendegúz's family the roles are reversed. His mother works full-time as an engineer — she is the breadwinner —, while his father stays at home: he cooks, does the shopping and takes the children to school. At first some neighbours were surprised, but now they say it is the happiest family in the street. His parents both say the secret is that they share everything, the work and the money as well.

### c) Nemzedékek együttélése – a barátod nagyapja

> Two years ago my friend's grandfather moved in with them after his surgery. At the beginning it was difficult: he wanted to watch TV all day and complained about the loud music. But now he is the "boss" of the household: he helps the children with their homework and teaches them chess. My friend says living with grandpa is like living with a walking history book — you just have to be patient with him.

### d) Egy teljes tétel egyetlen családról – a nagybátyád családja

> I would like to introduce my uncle's family, because they show all three points of this topic.
>
> First, family models. They are a large family with five children, so there is always something happening in their house — in my opinion, their kids will never be lonely.
>
> As for the division of labour, my uncle works as a bus driver, and my aunt works part-time in a shop. Even the smallest child has a job at home: he feeds the dog, while the others do the washing-up and take turns with the vacuuming.
>
> Finally, generations. My great-aunt, who is over eighty, lives with them too. She can't do the chores any more, but the whole family says her stories and advice are worth more than her help.
>
> All things considered, they are the noisiest but happiest family I know. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Készülj konkrét adatokkal: „There are four of us", „my sister is ten years old", „we see our cousins every second Sunday" – a pontos, folyékony leírás nyelvi pontot ér.
- Szükséd lesz múlt időre is: „When I was small, …", „My grandmother used to live in the countryside."
- A „generations living together" pontnál mindenképp mondd el az előnyöket ÉS a hátrányokat (on the one hand … on the other hand).
- Ne felejtsd el a birtokos szerkezeteket: *my father's car, my parents' house* – családról beszélve gyakran kell.
