# Man and society – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Man and society**

- volunteering
- communities (association, club, online/offline groups)
- public safety

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Volunteering

- Have you ever done any volunteer work?
- Why do people volunteer?
- Which organizations or causes would you like to help?
- Can volunteering help your career?

### Communities

- Are you a member of any club or association?
- What are the advantages of being part of a community?
- What do you think about online communities?
- Would you like to start a club yourself?

### Public safety

- Do you feel safe in your town?
- What crimes are the most common in your area?
- What can we do to prevent crime?
- Is it safe to travel home alone at night?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

- **First of all, … / To begin with, …** – Mindenekelőtt…
- **Another thing is that …** – Egy másik dolog az, hogy…
- **On the one hand … on the other hand …** – Egyrészt… másrészt…
- **What is more, … / Besides, …** – Ráadásul…
- **All things considered, …** – Mindent egybevetve…
- **In my opinion / Personally, I think …** – Véleményem szerint…
- **It depends on …** – Attól függ, hogy…

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about man and society: volunteering, communities, and public safety.
>
> First of all, volunteering. I have done volunteer work several times: our class collects food and toys for poor families at Christmas, and I sometimes help my sister at the animal shelter, where she feeds the dogs and cleans their kennels. I think people volunteer because helping others makes them feel useful. What is more, volunteering can even help your career, because you learn teamwork and responsibility.
>
> As for communities, I am a member of a football club and a scout group. In my opinion, communities are important because they teach cooperation and give you friends for life. I also belong to online groups, for example our class group, which is useful for homework — although I believe online communities cannot fully replace real ones.
>
> Finally, public safety. I feel quite safe in my town: there are CCTV cameras in the centre, and the police patrol the streets at night. Of course, theft happens, so we lock our bikes and avoid badly lit streets after dark.
>
> All things considered, I believe that if everybody gave a helping hand, our society would be safer and happier. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Önkéntesség – a nővéred

> My sister has been a volunteer at the local animal shelter for two years. Every second Saturday she feeds the dogs, walks them and cleans the kennels. She also helps to raise money: last year the shelter organised a charity run, and she collected over a hundred thousand forints. She says the work is tiring, but it makes her really happy when a dog finds a new family. Thanks to her, I donate my old clothes to charities instead of throwing them away.

### b) Közösségek – a nagypapád és az unokatesóved

> My grandfather has been a member of the local volunteer fire-brigade association for forty years. They organise events, teach children about fire safety, and my grandmother jokes that the association is his second family. My cousin, on the other hand, belongs to an online gaming community with members from all over Europe. They play together every evening, and they even meet offline once a year in Budapest. So I believe that even an online group can become a real community.

### c) Közbiztonság – egy konkrét eset a szomszédból

> Last year my neighbour's flat was broken into while they were on holiday. The burglars took the TV and some jewellery, but a CCTV camera at the entrance helped the police — they caught the burglars within a week. Since then our street has a neighbourhood chat group where people warn each other about strangers, and the local council installed more cameras. My neighbour says the biggest lesson is simple: always lock the door twice, and never hide a spare key outside.

### d) Egy teljes tétel egyetlen személyről – az unokatesóved, a rendőr

> I would like to talk about my cousin Ricsi, who is a police officer, because his life is all about society.
>
> First, volunteering. Before he joined the police, he volunteered as a football coach for children, and he still trains a children's team for free at weekends.
>
> As for communities, he is a member of the police sports club. His team organises charity runs and blood-donation days, so he is a well-known and popular person in our town.
>
> Speaking about public safety, he says the most common crimes here are bike thefts and car break-ins. His advice is simple: lock your bike to something solid, never leave valuables in the car, and walk on well-lit streets at night.
>
> All things considered, Ricsi shows that one person really can make a difference in a community. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Ez a tétel kiváló alkalom a feltételes mondatokra: „If everybody helped, our town would be safer." – ez nyelvtani pontot is hoz.
- A közbiztonságnál említs konkrét tanácsokat (lock your bike, well-lit streets, CCTV) – a gyakorlati tartalom hiteles.
- Az online közösségeknél mutasd meg az eltérő véleményt: előny ÉS hátrány (fake news, időrabló).
- Ha a vizsgáztató elkalauzol (pl. „Should prisoners work?" vagy „Is punishment or education more important?"), maradj nyugodt, és védd a véleményedet indokokkal.
