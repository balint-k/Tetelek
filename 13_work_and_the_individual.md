# Work and the individual – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Work and the individual**

- career choice
- working from home, summer job, student work
- working abroad (advantages and disadvantages)
- career and personal life

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Career choice

- What career would you like to choose and why?
- Who or what influences career choice?
- Is money important in a job? How much?
- What job would you never do?

### Working from home, summer job, student work

- Have you ever had a summer or student job?
- What did you learn from it?
- What do you think about working from home?
- Should students work during the school year?

### Working abroad

- Why do so many people work abroad?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages?
- Would you like to work abroad one day?
- What problems can foreign workers face?

### Career and personal life

- How can work and family life be balanced?
- Which is more important: a career or a family?
- What is work-life balance?
- Can too much work damage your health?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about work and the individual: career choice, student work, working abroad and free time.
>
> First of all, career choice. I would like to be a software developer, because I love computers, and I am quite good at maths. Of course, my parents have an opinion too, but they say the decision is mine. I think enjoying your job is more important than earning a lot — but honestly, a well-paid job is also welcome.
>
> As for student work, last summer I had a part-time job in a supermarket at the weekends. It was tiring, but it taught me the value of money — since then, I think twice before spending it. About working from home: my sister works as an accountant from home, and she says it is flexible and saves the commuting time, but it is hard to switch off when your office is your kitchen.
>
> Speaking about working abroad, my cousin works in Germany. The advantages are clear: a much higher salary and great experience. On the other hand, he is often homesick, and he can visit us only twice a year. If I worked abroad, I would miss my family too — but maybe for a few years it would be worth it.
>
> Finally, career and personal life. I think work-life balance is extremely important: a career should never cost you your health or your family.
>
> All things considered, work is important, but it should serve your life — not the other way round. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Pályaválasztás – a nővéred, aki ápolónőnek ment

> When my sister announced that she wanted to be a nurse, everybody was surprised — she had the best maths results in her class, and nursing is hard and not very well paid. But she said it was her calling: she loves helping people, and she is calm in every crisis. My grandmother, who was a nurse herself, told her: "You will be tired, but you will never be bored." Three years later she works in a hospital, she is exhausted after every shift — and she has never regretted her choice for a single day.

### b) Diákmunka – a barátod nyara az eperföldön

> Last summer my friend Bendegúz worked at a strawberry farm for two months. He got up at four in the morning, picked fruit for eight hours in the sun, and came home with red hands and a red back. He earned his first own money there, and he says it changed him: before, he spent his parents' money in a day; after the farm, he counted every forint. His only luxury was a weekly pizza — and he says no pizza has ever tasted that good since.

### c) Külföldi munka – az unokatesóved Németországban

> My cousin Gabi moved to Germany four years ago, and his story has two sides. The bright side: he earns almost three times the Hungarian salary, he bought a car, and he sends money home to his parents. The dark side: at the beginning he barely spoke German, so he felt lonely and stupid; he missed every family birthday and his grandmother's Sunday lunches. Now he has adapted — he has friends there and speaks the language —, but he still says: "The money is good, but the kitchen table at home is better."

### d) Karrier és magánélet – az édesapád, a munkamániás

> A few years ago my father was a real workaholic: overtime every week, phone calls at dinner, and he even missed my birthday because of a project. Then his doctor warned him about his blood pressure, and my mother told him: "You are earning money for a family that never sees you." It was a shock, but it worked: now he leaves the office at four, plays football with me on Fridays, and turns off his phone at dinner. He says he earns a bit less, but he is richer — and we all feel the difference.

### e) Egy teljes tétel – az unokatesóved, a németországi autószerelő

> I would like to tell you the story of my cousin Gabi, because his working life shows every point of this topic.
>
> First, career choice. He chose to be a car mechanic, because — as he says — "petrol runs in the family": my grandfather was a mechanic too.
>
> As for student work, he spent every summer holiday in my grandfather's garage, cleaning parts and watching the masters work. He earned his first pocket money there.
>
> After his vocational school he decided to work abroad, in Germany. The advantages: high salary, modern workshops and great experience. The disadvantages: at first, the language barrier and terrible homesickness.
>
> And career versus personal life: for years his only love was the garage, but now he manages his time better — he has a girlfriend here, video-chats with the family every Sunday, and comes home every Christmas.
>
> All things considered, Gabi taught me that a career is a marathon, not a sprint — and that family is the finish line. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Használj feltételes mondatokat: *If I worked abroad, I would miss my family.* – ez a tétel ideális terepe a 2. feltételnek.
- Legyen kész a pályaválasztásod „miértje": *because I'm good at…, because I love…* + egy konkrét terv.
- A külföldi munkánál mindig mondd el az ELŐNYÖT és a HÁTRÁNYT is – a vizsgáztató erre vár.
- Számokkal dolgozz: fizetés, munkaórák – de ne sorold csak az adatokat, hanem ágyazd mondatba őket.
