## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about travel: preparations, destinations, and the choice between organised and individual trips.
>
> First of all, travel arrangements. Before a trip, my family always books the accommodation and the tickets online, checks the weather forecast, and packs only the most important things. Of course, we never forget the documents: passports or ID cards, and travel insurance, because anything can happen on the road.
>
> As for destinations, my favourite holiday was in Greece two years ago: we stayed by the sea, swam all day, and did sightseeing in the evenings. In Hungary, I would recommend Lake Balaton for the summer and Budapest for a city trip — the Parliament and the Castle are worth a whole weekend.
>
> Speaking about organised and individual travel: a package holiday is comfortable — the travel agency arranges everything, and you just relax. On the other hand, backpacking is cheaper and more flexible: you can change your plans any day. Both have risks: package tourists can end up in tourist traps, and backpackers must solve every problem alone.
>
> All things considered, I would choose an organised trip with my family and a backpacking tour with my friends — the perfect holiday depends on the company. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Utazásszervezés – az édesapád, a csomagolás főnöke

> For my father, travelling starts two weeks before the trip — with a checklist. He prints the bookings, takes photos of the passports "for safety", and arrives at the airport three hours early, while everyone else is still at breakfast. His masterpiece is packing: one suitcase, no wrinkles, and a spare bag folded inside "for souvenirs". There was only one moment when his system failed: on our trip to Spain, his own suitcase stayed in Frankfurt, and for two days the great organiser wore my mother's T-shirts. Now even he admits: you can plan everything, except the luggage system of a big airport.

### b) Úti célok – a nagymamád és a Balaton

> My grandmother has spent her summer holidays at Lake Balaton for fifty-five years — same town, same street, almost same house. When my cousin showed her photos of Bali, she nodded politely and said: "Very nice. Does it have a csárda where they know your name?" For her, a holiday is not about distance but about routine: the same bench, the same sunset, and the same street musicians every August. Some people say she is missing the world — but maybe the world is missing a grandmother who knows exactly where her happiness lives.

### c) Egyéni vs. szervezett utazás – a család két tábora

> Our family is divided into two travel camps. My aunt and uncle are package people: they love their all-inclusive hotel, the sunbed reserved at six in the morning, and the buffet where nobody can surprise them. Their children are the exact opposite: last summer they went backpacking with hostels and Interrail tickets, and they say the best moments were the unplanned ones — a village festival they found by accident and a night train where they made friends from three countries. Both sides swear their way is the only way — but at Christmas, they all listen to the backpackers' stories first.

### d) Egy teljes tétel – az unokatesóved Interrail-utazása

> I would like to tell you about my cousin Dóri's Interrail trip last summer, because it is the perfect example of modern travel.
>
> First, the arrangements. She booked the train pass online months ahead, planned only the first three nights, and packed everything into one backpack — including her grandmother's sun cream.
>
> Her destinations were flexible by design: Vienna, Munich, Milan and Barcelona, with two surprise stops that "just happened".
>
> Speaking about individual travel, the advantages are clear: freedom, low costs and hostels full of friends. The disadvantages appeared too: one night train that never came, a lost phone in Milan, and a backpack that got heavier every day.
>
> When she came home, she had no souvenirs — only stories, a full notebook and a German friend who will visit us next spring.
>
> All things considered, Dóri's trip taught me that the best itinerary is the one that leaves space for surprises. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Elöljárók: *arrive IN a city, arrive AT the airport, travel BY plane, be ON holiday* – ezekből a vizsgáztató szívesen kérdez.
- Készülj egy "legjobb nyaralam" történettel 5-6 mondatban, múlt időben – ez szinte kötelező elem.
- A csomagolásnál használd a felsorolást: *I always pack sun cream, a swimming suit and a book* – ez természetes nyelvi pont.
- Gyakorold a preferenciát: *I'd rather…, I prefer… to…, …is more exciting than…*
