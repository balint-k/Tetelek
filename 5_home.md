# Home – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Home**

- the examinee's home
- housing culture, interior design
- household chores and machinery
- housing-related costs, home maintenance
- the ideal home

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### The examinee's home

- Describe your home. Where do you live?
- Which is your favourite room and why?
- What can you see from your window?
- What would you change in your flat or house?

### Housing culture, interior design

- How is your room decorated?
- Do you prefer a modern or a traditional style? Why?
- What furniture is there in your room?
- Do you like decorating or doing DIY?

### Household chores and machinery

- What chores do you do at home?
- What household appliances do you use?
- Which machine is the most useful in your opinion?
- Do you think children should help with the chores?

### Housing-related costs, home maintenance

- What costs does a home have?
- Who pays the bills in your family?
- What can we do to reduce the costs?
- What do you do when something breaks down?

### The ideal home

- What would your ideal home be like?
- Would you like to live in a house or in a flat?
- Where would your ideal home be?
- What rooms would it have?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

- **First of all, … / To begin with, …** – Mindenekelőtt…
- **Another thing is that …** – Egy másik dolog az, hogy…
- **On the one hand … on the other hand …** – Egyrészt… másrészt…
- **What is more, … / Besides, …** – Ráadásul…
- **All things considered, …** – Mindent egybevetve…
- **In my opinion / Personally, I think …** – Véleményem szerint…
- **It depends on …** – Attól függ, hogy…

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about my home: what it looks like, the chores and costs, and my ideal home.
>
> First of all, I live with my parents in a second-floor flat in a block of flats. There are three rooms, a kitchen and a balcony. My favourite room is my own room: there is a desk by the window, a big bookshelf and posters on the wall. From my window I can see the park, which is lovely in the morning.
>
> As for interior design, our flat is furnished in a simple, modern style — we prefer it to old-fashioned, heavy furniture. I find our living room really cosy because of the warm lights and the plants.
>
> Speaking about chores, everybody does something in our family. I do the washing-up and take out the rubbish, and my favourite machine is the dishwasher, because it saves us a lot of time. We also have a washing machine and a robot vacuum cleaner.
>
> Of course, a home costs money. My parents pay the utility bills every month — electricity, water and gas —, and they took a mortgage to buy the flat. To reduce the costs, we use LED bulbs and turn off the lights when we leave a room. When something breaks down, my father tries to repair it himself first.
>
> Finally, my ideal home. It would be a detached house with a small garden near a forest, with a fireplace, a study and a big dog in the garden.
>
> All things considered, I love my flat, but I hope that one day my dream house will come true. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Barátod háza a faluban

> My friend Bendegúz lives in a big family house in a village. It has two floors, six rooms and a huge garden with fruit trees. His room is in the attic, and from his window he can see the whole street and the hills behind the village. The house is furnished in a traditional style — old, heavy furniture and carpets everywhere — but it is really cosy. I always feel at home there, and honestly, sometimes I envy him for the garden.

### b) Bel lakás – a nagymamád és a nénikéd összevetése

> My grandmother's flat and my aunt's flat could not be more different. My grandmother's home looks like a museum of the seventies: patterned wallpaper, doilies on the shelves and family photos on every wall. My aunt, on the other hand, loves minimalist design: white furniture, plain walls and no clutter at all. I like both, in a different way — my grandmother's flat is warm and full of memories, while my aunt's is elegant and easy to clean.

### c) Házimunka és gépek – a nagypapád, aki nem hisz a gépekben

> My grandfather is eighty, and he doesn't trust household machines at all. He says the dishwasher "wastes water", so he washes up by hand, and he still mows the lawn with an old, hand-push mower. The only machine he really likes is his ancient radio. My mother keeps telling him that the machines would give him more free time, but he answers that working with his hands keeps him young — and maybe he is right.

### d) Költségek és karbantartás – egy emlékezetes téli este

> Two years ago, in the coldest week of winter, the boiler in our block of flats broke down. There was no hot water for three days, and the flat got really cold, so we slept in sleeping bags. My father called the repairman every day, and the neighbours finally collected money together to pay for the repair. Since then my parents always say that maintenance is cheaper than repairing — a home is like a car, you have to take care of it.

### e) Egy teljes tétel – a nagyszüleid háza

> I would like to describe my grandparents' house in the village, because for me it is the perfect example of what "home" means.
>
> First, the house itself. It is a one-storey family house with a porch, a big kitchen and three rooms. The windows look onto the garden, and there is a swing under the old walnut tree.
>
> As for interior design, the house is furnished in traditional country style: a painted wooden wardrobe, hand-made tablecloths and photos of the family everywhere. It is old-fashioned, but it is the cosiest place I know.
>
> Speaking about chores and machines, my grandmother still does the laundry by hand for her delicate clothes, and the only "machine" my grandfather needs is his tractor. They both work in the garden every day.
>
> The costs are low there: they heat with wood from their own forest, and they grow most of their food themselves.
>
> Finally, the ideal home. When I asked my grandmother, she said this house has been her dream home for fifty years — she wouldn't change it for any modern flat in the city.
>
> All things considered, their house taught me that a home is not about luxury but about love. Thank you for listening.

> **Megjegyzés:** az „examinee's home" pont lényege, hogy a *saját* lakásodról kell beszélned – ezt nem lehet másra áthárítani. A fenti példák a stílust és a szókincset mutatják; a szinteket, szobák számát és részleteket mindenképp írd át a valóságodra.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Tanuld fejből a saját lakásod 5–6 mondatos leírását – ebből a tételből ez biztosan elhangzik.
- Használj helymeghatározó elöljárókat: *opposite the door, next to the window, on the left, on the ground floor* – ezek strukturáltságot adnak.
- Készülj számokkal: hányadik emelet, hány szoba, hány négyzetméter (*a 60-square-metre flat*).
- A költségeknél mutass tudatosságot: az energiatakarékosság (LED bulbs, turn off the lights) mindig jó téma.
