# Housing – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Housing**

- urban and rural lifestyles
- housing conditions (property, rent, sublet, residence hall)
- apartment, family house
- the surroundings

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Urban and rural lifestyles

- What are the advantages and disadvantages of living in a city?
- Would you rather live in a village or in a city? Why?
- Why do so many young people move to cities?
- What is commuting like?

### Housing conditions

- Is it better to rent a flat or to buy one?
- What are the advantages of living in a residence hall?
- What is a sublet like?
- How difficult is it to buy property in Hungary?

### Apartment, family house

- Compare living in a flat and in a family house.
- Which would you prefer as an adult? Why?
- Why are family houses so popular in Hungary?
- What are the disadvantages of a big house?

### The surroundings

- Describe your neighbourhood.
- What services are there near your home?
- What makes a neighbourhood pleasant?
- How important are green areas for you?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about housing: city and village life, renting and buying, and the surroundings.
>
> First of all, urban and rural lifestyles. I live in a medium-sized town, and I like the calm and the fresh air here. My friend from Budapest always says the city is exciting — there are more jobs, cinemas and restaurants —, but the flats are expensive, the air is polluted and life is stressful. On the other hand, villages are cheap and quiet, but you have to commute to work, and there are fewer opportunities. So it depends on what stage of life you are in.
>
> As for housing conditions, most young people cannot afford to buy property in Hungary, so they rent a flat or sublet a room, and students can live in a residence hall, which is the cheapest option. Buying a flat needs a mortgage for many years.
>
> Speaking about flats and family houses: a flat is easier to maintain, but a house gives you more space and a garden. When I grow up, I would prefer a family house in a suburb.
>
> Finally, the surroundings. Our street is quiet and leafy, with a playground, a corner shop and a bus stop nearby, which makes our neighbourhood really pleasant.
>
> All things considered, both lifestyles have advantages, but for me a house in a green suburb would be the perfect choice. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Városi vs. vidéki élet – az unokatesóved, aki Budapestre költözött

> My cousin Anna moved from our small town to Budapest two years ago, because she got a job at a design company. She loves the city life: concerts, metro, coffee shops open till midnight. But she also complains a lot — her flat is tiny, the rent takes half of her salary, and she can't sleep because of the traffic. When she comes home for Christmas, she says the silence of our town is the biggest luxury, but after a week she already misses the city.

### b) Lakhatás – a nővéred diákévei

> My sister tried almost every kind of student housing. In her first year she lived in a residence hall: the room was small and shared, but it was cheap and she made her best friends there. Later she and two classmates rented a flat near the university. The landlord was kind, but they had to pay two months' rent as a deposit, and the bills were higher than they expected. She says a sublet is good for one semester, but for a whole year it's better to rent properly.

### c) Lakás vs. családi ház – a nagyszüleid és a szüleid összevetése

> My grandparents have lived in their own family house for fifty years, while my parents bought a flat in a block. My grandfather says he could never live "in a box in the sky": he needs his garden, his workshop and a place where nobody can hear him. My father answers that the house is beautiful, but it needs constant work — in the flat, the stairs get cleaned themselves, and there's no lawn to mow, no snow to shovel, and no roof to repair.

### d) A környék – a barátod pesti környéke

> My friend lives in the thirteenth district of Budapest, and honestly, his neighbourhood is more exciting than our whole town. There is a shopping mall, a cinema and a huge park within ten minutes' walk. Of course, there are disadvantages too: the streets are noisy, the air is worse, and his mother doesn't like walking home alone at night. Still, he says he would never move back to a small town, because "everything is within reach".

### e) Egy teljes tétel – a bátyád első lakása

> I would like to tell you the story of my older brother's first flat, because it shows everything about housing.
>
> First, the lifestyle. He moved from our town to the capital, because he found a good job there, and he fell in love with the fast pace of city life.
>
> As for housing conditions, in the beginning he sublet a single room, because buying property was impossible for him. Two years later he and his girlfriend rented a small flat: the deposit was two months' rent, and the estate agent's fee was another one.
>
> The flat itself is on the fourth floor of a block of flats, forty square metres, with one bedroom and a tiny balcony. They chose a flat, not a house, because it is cheaper to maintain.
>
> The surroundings make up for the size: there is a park opposite the building, a corner shop next door and the metro two streets away.
>
> All things considered, my brother says it is not the flat that matters, but where it is and who you share it with. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Ez az összehasonlítás tételsora: használj fokozást és hasonlítást (*cheaper than, more convenient than, not as quiet as, the most expensive*).
- Készülj árakról szóló kifejezésekkel: *it costs…, the rent is…, it takes half of his salary* – a számok hitelesítik a választ.
- Tipikus rákérdezés: „Would you like to move? Why / why not?" – legyen kész 3–4 mondatos véleményed.
- Ne keverd a szókincset: *rent* (bérlés), *flat/apartment* (lakás), *storey* (szint), *floor* – használd következetesen.
