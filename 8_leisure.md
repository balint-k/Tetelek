# Leisure – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Leisure**

- reading (non-digital, digital)
- films, videos, series
- reading or watching a screen
- movies at home or in the cinema

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Reading

- Do you like reading? What do you read?
- Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?
- What was the last book you read?
- What was your favourite fairy tale as a small child?

### Films, videos, series

- What kind of films do you like?
- What series are you watching at the moment?
- How much time do you spend in front of a screen?
- Who is your favourite actor or YouTuber?

### Reading or watching a screen

- Is reading better than watching films? Why?
- Do you think people read less than before?
- Have you ever seen a film that was based on a book? Which was better?
- What do you think about audiobooks?

### Movies at home or in the cinema

- Do you prefer watching films at home or in the cinema?
- How often do you go to the cinema?
- What was the last film you saw?
- What are the advantages of the cinema and of home viewing?

## 4. Összekötő kifejezések (Linking phrases)

- **First of all, … / To begin with, …** – Mindenekelőtt…
- **Another thing is that …** – Egy másik dolog az, hogy…
- **On the one hand … on the other hand …** – Egyrészt… másrészt…
- **What is more, … / Besides, …** – Ráadásul…
- **All things considered, …** – Mindent egybevetve…
- **In my opinion / Personally, I think …** – Véleményem szerint…
- **It depends on …** – Attól függ, hogy…

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about my free time: reading, watching films and series, and the cinema.
>
> First of all, reading. I read mostly fantasy novels, and I am a real page-turner fan — if a book is exciting, I cannot put it down. I prefer paper books to e-books, because I love the smell of a new book, and a shelf full of books looks great in my room. I usually borrow books from the school library.
>
> As for films and series, I like comedies and sci-fi. At the moment I am watching a detective series, and I always watch it with English subtitles, which really helps my English. Of course, I know I spend too much time in front of a screen.
>
> Speaking about reading or watching: in my opinion, books develop your imagination, while films show you everything ready-made. That is why I think the book is almost always better than the film. On the other hand, watching something is quicker and you can do it together with friends.
>
> Finally, home or cinema. I love the cinema — the huge screen, the sound, the popcorn —, but the tickets are expensive, so I go only for blockbusters. At home it is cheaper and more comfortable, and I can press pause any time.
>
> All things considered, I think the best solution is a balance: a good book during the week and a film at the weekend. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Olvasás – a nagymamád, a könyvmoly

> My grandmother is a real bookworm. She reads every single evening — mostly detective novels and romantic stories —, and she has been a member of the local library for fifty years. She says an e-reader would ruin the magic of reading, so her flat is full of paper books, from the kitchen to the balcony. My brother is the exact opposite: he hasn't finished a book since primary school, but he reads football news on his phone all day. When they argue about it, my grandmother always wins with one sentence: "A book never runs out of battery."

### b) Filmek, sorozatok – a nővéred sorozatestéi

> My sister is a big series fan. Her favourite is a Korean crime series, and she watches it with subtitles, never dubbed — she says the original voices are part of the atmosphere. She knows everything about the actors, and when a new season comes out, she binge-watches the whole thing in one weekend. My mother complains that she is glued to the screen, but honestly, thanks to the subtitles, my sister speaks better English than anyone in our family.

### c) Könyv vagy film – a barátod összehasonlítása

> Last year my friend Bendegúz read a famous fantasy novel and then watched the film version, so he could compare them. He said the film was visually amazing, but it had to leave out half of the plot — the book explained the characters much better, and it took the story slower. Now he always says: "Read the book first, watch the film second, and you won't be disappointed." I think he is right: if you watch the film first, you can't even imagine the characters yourself.

### d) Mozi vagy otthon – az apukád, a mozirajongó

> My father is a cinema fan — he says a film is only a real film on the big screen. He books the seats online weeks before the premiere, and he always buys the biggest popcorn. Last month he took me to a 3D sci-fi blockbuster, and I have to admit, the sound and the effects were unbelievable. My sister never joins us: she prefers streaming films at home in her pyjamas, with the pause button in her hand. So in our family we have both types — and I understand both.

### e) Egy teljes tétel – a barátod, Bendegúz szabadideje

> I would like to tell you about my friend Bendegúz's leisure time, because he is the perfect example of this topic.
>
> First, reading. He doesn't read classic novels, but he reads a lot in his own way: comics, football news and articles online — so reading doesn't always mean a paper book.
>
> As for films and series, he watches action films and football highlights, and he is a huge fan of a comedy series, which he knows by heart.
>
> Speaking about reading versus watching: he read the famous fantasy novel last year, watched the film after that, and — surprisingly — he became a reader. He says comparing the book and the film was the most fun he has had in years.
>
> Finally, home or cinema. For normal films he stays at home, but for blockbusters he always goes to the cinema, because "a big film needs a big screen".
>
> All things considered, Bendegúz taught me that there is no bad hobby — reading and watching can live happily together. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Műfajnevekkel készülj: *thriller, comedy, sci-fi, documentary* – és mindegyikhez egy „miért".
- Élménybeszámolónál figyelj az igeidőkre: *I have seen…* (általában) vs *Last year I watched…* (konkrét múlt).
- A „book vs film" kérdésre a nyerő válaszszerkezet: „In most cases the book is better, because…" + egy példa.
- Használj gyakorisági határozókat: *twice a month, now and then, almost every evening* – ezeket a vizsgáztató keresi.
