# Culture, entertainment – B szintű szóbeli tétel

## 1. Tétel és vezérfonal

**Topic: Culture, entertainment**

- music (pop and classical music)
- theatre
- festivals
- museum, exhibitions

---

## 2. Vezető kérdések (vizsgáztatói kérdések)

### Music

- What kind of music do you listen to?
- Do you play an instrument? Would you like to?
- Do you prefer pop or classical music? Why?
- Have you ever been to a concert?

### Theatre

- Have you ever been to the theatre?
- What was the performance about?
- Do you think theatre is old-fashioned?
- Would you like to work in a theatre one day?

### Festivals

- What festivals do you know in Hungary?
- Have you ever been to a music festival?
- What are the advantages and dangers of festivals?
- Would you like to go to the Sziget Festival?

### Museum, exhibitions

- Do you like museums?
- What was the last exhibition you saw?
- Why should young people visit museums?
- Would you try a virtual museum tour?

## 5. Minta felelet (Sample monologue)

> Today I would like to talk about culture and entertainment: music, theatre, festivals and museums.
>
> First of all, music. I mostly listen to pop music — I stream my favourite bands every day while doing my homework. I don't play an instrument yet, but I would love to learn the guitar. My mother plays the piano and loves classical music, and I have to admit, a live orchestra concert is a completely different experience.
>
> As for theatre, last year our class went to see a comedy. It was much funnier live than anything on a screen — the actors, the costumes, the laughter of the audience. In my opinion, theatre is not old-fashioned at all; it is just harder to get young people off their phones.
>
> Speaking about festivals, my cousin goes to the Sziget Festival every summer. He says the atmosphere is unforgettable: the headliners, the camping, meeting people from all over Europe. On the other hand, the tickets are expensive, the mud is deep when it rains, and you have to be careful at big crowds.
>
> Finally, museums. My favourite exhibition last year was about the history of video games — I think museums just need topics that young people love.
>
> All things considered, I believe culture is not boring — you only have to find the door that leads you in. Thank you for listening.

## 6. Minta válaszok – ha másvalakiről beszélsz

### a) Zene – az unokatesóved zenekara

> My cousin Márk plays the bass in a garage band with three of his classmates. They rehearse every Friday, and last month they had their first real concert at a school party. My aunt says she hasn't seen him so proud since he was born. They play a mix of rock and punk, and they even wrote two songs of their own — the lyrics are about school and girls. He says being in a band taught him more about teamwork than any group project at school.

### b) Színház – a nagynénéd, a színházi bérletes

> My aunt has had a season ticket to the local theatre for fifteen years, and she never misses a premiere. She can talk about actors, costumes and stage design for hours, and she keeps all the programmes in a box at home. She always says the same thing: "In a film, the actor can repeat the scene ten times. In the theatre, everything happens only once — that is the magic." When I went with her once, I understood what she meant: the silence of the audience before the curtain went up was magical.

### c) Fesztivál – az unokatesóved Sziget-élménye

> My cousin has been to the Sziget Festival three times, and every August he comes home with the same stories: his tent almost flew away in a storm, the mud was knee-deep, and he queued an hour for a shower. But when he talks about the concerts, his eyes shine — he saw his favourite band live, and he made friends from Spain and Finland. He saves money for the ticket months ahead. He says one week on the island is worth a whole summer, and honestly, I believe him.

### d) Múzeum – a nagypapád és a kistesóved

> My grandfather takes my little cousin to the Natural History Museum almost every month. At first I thought it was boring for a small child, but I was wrong: the boy is obsessed with the dinosaur skeletons and asks a hundred questions, and my grandfather answers like a professional guide. Last time they also tried the new VR exhibition, and now my grandfather says modern technology and museums are the perfect pair. It is their special grandfather–grandson programme, and I think it is better than any cartoon.

### e) Egy teljes tétel – az édesanyád, a kulturális ember

> I would like to talk about my mother, because in our family she is the person who really lives for culture.
>
> First, music. She plays the piano, and every Sunday morning the flat is full of classical music — Chopin is her favourite. She says pop music is for the bus, but classical music is for the soul.
>
> As for theatre, she has a season ticket, and she takes me to a family performance once or twice a year.
>
> Speaking about festivals, she is not a camping type, but every summer we go to a local wine and music festival together, and she dances even without wine.
>
> Finally, museums: she plans a museum visit for every birthday — this way, she says, culture is a gift that never gets old.
>
> All things considered, my mother taught me that culture is not a school subject, but a lifestyle. Thank you for listening.

## 7. Tippek a vizsgára (Exam tips)

- Legyen egy konkrét élményed, amit múlt időben el tudsz mesélni (koncert, színházi látogatás, kiállítás) – dátummal, szereplőkkel.
- Használd a *live* szót helyesen: *a live concert, the actors were live on stage* – ez adja a tétel lényegét.
- A fesztiváloknál gyűjts előnyt ÉS hátrányt (atmosphere vs. mud, crowds, price).
- Zárd mindig véleménnyel: „In my opinion, everyone should try theatre once."
